Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

My Drive Sync uses rclone to access your Google Drive so it can upload, download and sync your files.

What data we access

Files and folders in your Google Drive, only as needed to perform the sync you request.

How we use it

Your data is used only to transfer files between your device and your Google Drive. It is not stored on any server, sold, or shared with third parties.

Storage

The access token is stored locally on your device in the rclone config file. Nothing is sent anywhere except Google.

Revoking access

You can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
